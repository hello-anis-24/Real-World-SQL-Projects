## 📁 Detailed Case Studies

### 01. IPL Data Analysis 🏏

**Business Objective:** Analyze player salaries, team spending, and player distribution to understand team investment strategies.

Key business questions:
- What is the total spending of each team?
- Who are the highest-paid all-rounders?
- Who is the most expensive player in each team?
- What percentage of team spending goes to each player?
- How are players distributed across salary categories?

**SQL Concepts:** GROUP BY, CTEs, CASE, Window Functions, RANK(), Subqueries.

### 02. T20I Cricket Match Analysis 🏆

**Business Objective:** Evaluate international cricket team performance, winning patterns, and match statistics.

Key business questions:
- Which team achieved the most wins?
- What are the head-to-head records between teams?
- Which teams have the highest average winning margins?
- What is each team's win percentage?
- Which teams perform best at different grounds?

**SQL Concepts:** Aggregate Functions, CTEs, DENSE_RANK(), Date Functions, String Functions, Subqueries.

### 03. Flight Operations Analysis ✈️

**Business Objective:** Analyze airline operations, airport activity, passenger travel patterns, and ticket sales.

Key business questions:
- Which airport handles the highest number of flights?
- How many tickets are sold by each airline?
- Which airline operates the most flights from each airport?
- What are the most expensive tickets across different routes?
- Who are the highest-spending passengers?

**SQL Concepts:** Multiple JOINs, CTEs, Window Functions, DATEDIFF(), Ranking and Aggregation.

### 04. Hospital Data Analysis 🏥

**Business Objective:** Analyze hospital operations, patient visits, department revenue, doctor performance, and patient satisfaction.

Key business questions:
- Which doctors treat the highest number of distinct patients?
- Which departments generate the most revenue?
- How do patient visits differ between weekdays and weekends?
- What are the average waiting times and satisfaction scores?
- Which treatments are most frequently prescribed for different diagnoses?

**SQL Concepts:** Data Cleaning, Data Consolidation, CASE, CTEs, Window Functions, Ranking, Date Functions and Aggregation.

### 05. Superstore Sales Analysis 🛒

**Business Objective:** Evaluate retail sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product profitability, and sales targets.

Key business questions:
- Who are the top 10 customers by total sales?
- Which products have never been sold?
- How many new customers were acquired in a particular year?
- What is the profit margin for each category?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- How do actual sales compare with target sales?

**SQL Concepts:** CTEs, Subqueries, Correlated Subqueries, Window Functions, Running Totals, PIVOT/UNPIVOT and Conditional Aggregation.

## ▶️ How to Use This Repository

1. Clone or download this repository.

**Note:** Execute scripts in the appropriate order. Some scripts depend on tables created by earlier scripts.
